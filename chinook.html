Diagnosing Growth Stagnation

This case study details the optimization strategy built for the digital store database to solve stagnation, optimize marketing allocation, and prune under-performing assets.

✨ Revenue Retention

Reallocating local marketing budgets to high-retention regions and channels to lower customer acquisition cost (CAC) and increase customer lifetime value (LTV).

🗂️ Asset Pruning

Identifying zero-sales and low-performance digital music tracks and albums to free up host performance and lower licensing/database maintenance costs.

📐 Localized Pricing

Deploying localized discount matrices dynamically based on regional purchase power index (PPI) to maximize purchase frequencies in emerging markets.

🔁 Purchase Funnel Modifiers

Optimizing multi-genre playlist suggestions and building customized bundles to increase average order value (AOV) and cross-selling ratios.
